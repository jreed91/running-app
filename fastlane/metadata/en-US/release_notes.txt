See your running like never before, and share it your way.

• Progress, all on one page: filter by period and run type, tap a week to see its runs, and drag across any chart for the exact number.
• Every route on one map: a heatmap of everywhere you run. Filter by year or run type, tap a street to see the runs that went along it, and spot the new ground you broke.
• Best efforts and fitness: your fastest 1K, mile, 5K and more, found inside every run, plus fitness, fatigue and form from your training load.
• Run analysis: pace, heart rate and elevation on one timeline.
• Share studio: story, post, square and sticker cards, and a video of your route drawing itself in. Privacy zones hide where you start and finish.
• Not feeling your best? Ease off or pause your plan, and Stride brings you back gently.

Also new: share your goal race with a friend, Plan Replay, strength workouts from Apple Health, and faster loading for long run histories.
